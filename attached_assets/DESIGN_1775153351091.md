# Design System Document: The Warm Arena

## 1. Overview & Creative North Star: "The Digital Majlis"
This design system moves away from the cold, industrial aesthetic of typical sports applications. Our Creative North Star is **"The Digital Majlis"**—an editorial-inspired, premium community space that feels as warm as a sun-drenched arena and as reliable as a high-end social club. 

Instead of a generic grid, we utilize **Organic Asymmetry** and **Tonal Depth**. We break the "template" look by layering soft, paper-like surfaces and using extreme typographic contrast. This system prioritizes breathing room (white space) over structural lines, ensuring that the "Warm Arena" feels open, inviting, and sophisticated.

---

## 2. Colors & Surface Philosophy
The palette is rooted in earthiness. We use Material Design 3 logic but strip away the "default" feel by applying a **No-Line Rule**.

### Adaptive Sport Identity
The UI adapts based on the sport context, shifting the `primary` and `secondary` tokens to match the energy of the game:
- **Padel:** `#0288D1` (Electric Blue) - Precision and glass.
- **Football:** `#2E7D32` (Pitch Green) - Tradition and turf.
- **Tennis:** `#EF6C00` (Clay Orange) - Agility and heat.

### The "No-Line" & Surface Hierarchy
**Strict Directive:** 1px solid borders are prohibited for sectioning. 
- **Nesting Depth:** Boundaries are defined solely by background shifts. Place a `surface-container-lowest` (#ffffff) card on a `surface-container-low` (#f5f5dc) section.
- **Glassmorphism:** Use `surface` colors at 70% opacity with a `20px` backdrop blur for floating navigation bars or modal headers to create a "frosted" high-end feel.
- **Signature Textures:** Apply a subtle linear gradient (from `primary` to `primary_container`) on main action buttons to give them a tactile, "weighted" appearance.

---

## 3. Typography: The Editorial Voice
We pair the geometric stability of **Inter** with the elegant, calligraphic roots of **Cairo** for RTL support. 

- **Display & Headlines (Cairo):** Used for large, impactful statements. These should be set with tight letter spacing and bold weights to act as "graphic anchors" on the page.
- **Body & Labels (Inter/Cairo):** For body text, we prioritize legibility. `body-lg` is our workhorse. 
- **The Contrast Rule:** To achieve a premium look, use a high scale jump. Pair a `display-md` headline directly with `body-sm` metadata to create an intentional, rhythmic hierarchy.

---

## 4. Elevation & Depth: Tonal Layering
Traditional drop shadows are too "software-ish." We use **Ambient Shadows** and **Tonal Stacking**.

- **Layering Principle:** 
  1. Base: `surface` (#fbfbe2)
  2. Section: `surface-container-low` (#f5f5dc)
  3. Interactive Element: `surface-container-lowest` (#ffffff)
- **Ambient Shadows:** When an element must float (e.g., a "Book Now" FAB), use a shadow tinted with the `on-surface` color at 5% opacity with a `32px` blur. It should look like a soft glow, not a dark smudge.
- **Ghost Borders:** If accessibility requires a border, use `outline-variant` at **15% opacity**. Never 100%.

---

## 5. Components: The Primitive Set

### Buttons (Tactile & Grounded)
- **Primary:** Gradient from `primary` to `primary_container`. No border. `1.5rem` (xl) corner radius.
- **Secondary:** `surface-container-highest` background with `on-surface` text.
- **Tertiary:** Pure text with an underline that only appears on hover/active states.

### Cards & Lists (The "Breathable" List)
- **Rule:** Forbid divider lines. 
- **Implementation:** Use `8px` of vertical space between list items. Use a `surface-container-low` background for the entire list block to distinguish it from the main `surface`.
- **Imagery:** Strictly use headless, faceless vector silhouettes. These should be stylized with the `adaptive sport` colors to maintain the "Warm Arena" vibe.

### Inputs (Soft Focus)
- **Text Fields:** Use `surface-container-highest` with a bottom-only `outline` (2px) that activates to the `primary` adaptive color on focus.
- **Checkboxes/Radios:** Use `8px` (md) roundedness even for checkboxes to keep the "soft" visual language consistent.

### Custom Component: The "Match-Card"
A specialized container with a `surface-container-lowest` background, featuring a large `display-sm` score/time and a subtle `tertiary_container` "pill" for the category.

---

## 6. Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Use RTL Intent:** Design specifically for Cairo’s height. Cairo needs more line-height (leading) than Inter—increase `line-height` by 1.2x for Arabic.
- **Embrace the Beige:** Let the `#F5F5DC` breathe. It is the "warmth" of the arena.
- **Abstract the Player:** Use vector shadows and silhouettes. It makes the app feel universal and focused on the sport, not the individual.

### Don't:
- **No Harsh Blacks:** Never use `#000000`. Use `#212529` (Matte Black) for all text to keep the "editorial" softness.
- **No Standard Grids:** Avoid perfectly centered, symmetrical layouts for Hero sections. Offset the vector illustration to the left (in RTL) to create dynamic tension.
- **No Solid Dividers:** If you feel the urge to draw a line, add `16px` of padding instead.

---

## 7. Visual Policy: Vector Soul
To maintain "Trust and Warmth" without human photos:
- Vectors should use **rounded terminals** (no sharp points).
- Use **Overlapping Shapes:** A Padel racket overlapping a circle creates depth without needing 3D renders.
- **Color Blending:** Set vector illustrations to `Multiply` or `Overlay` blend modes against the beige background to make them feel "printed" onto the screen.
