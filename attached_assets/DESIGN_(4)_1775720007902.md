# Design System Document: High-End Editorial Sports Experience

## 1. Overview & Creative North Star: "The Kinetic Gallery"
This design system is built to move. It rejects the static, boxy constraints of traditional booking apps in favor of **The Kinetic Gallery**—a creative North Star that treats every court, match, and player profile like a premium editorial feature. 

We break the "template" look by utilizing extreme corner radii (`rounded-full`), aggressive typographic scales, and depth created through light refraction rather than lines. The goal is to make the user feel like they aren't just "booking a slot," but entering a professional sports arena. We use intentional asymmetry and overlapping elements to create a sense of forward motion and high energy.

---

## 2. Colors & Surface Philosophy
The palette is rooted in a high-contrast relationship between a void-like background and a hyper-kinetic neon.

### Tonal Foundations
*   **Background (`#0e0e0e`):** A deep, pressurized charcoal that serves as our canvas.
*   **Primary (`#c9fd2d`):** "Neon Lime." Use this sparingly for maximum impact—CTAs, active states, and critical highlights.
*   **Secondary (`#eae958`):** A muted, sophisticated yellow-gold used to add depth to gradients and secondary actions.

### The "No-Line" Rule
**Borders are strictly prohibited.** To section content, designers must use:
1.  **Tonal Shifts:** Place a `surface-container-high` card against a `surface` background.
2.  **Negative Space:** Use generous padding to allow content to breathe and define its own boundaries.
3.  **Glassmorphism:** Use `surface-container` with a `backdrop-filter: blur(20px)` and 60% opacity to create separation.

### Signature Textures
Avoid flat primary blocks. For main CTAs or Hero sections, apply a subtle linear gradient:
*   **Primary Kinetic Gradient:** `primary` (#c9fd2d) at 0% to `primary-container` (#b3e601) at 100%. This adds "soul" and mimics the sheen of high-performance sports gear.

---

## 3. Typography: Editorial Impact
We use **Inter** exclusively. Its neutrality allows our layout's geometry to shine, while its bold weights provide the "professional sports" authority.

*   **Display (Lg/Md/Sm):** Extra Bold (800). Use for "The Hook"—match scores, big headlines, or "BOOK NOW." Use tight letter-spacing (-0.02em) to create a "blocked" editorial look.
*   **Headline (Lg/Md/Sm):** Semi-Bold (600). Used for section titles. These should feel like magazine headers.
*   **Title (Lg/Md/Sm):** Medium (500). Used for card titles and navigation headers.
*   **Body (Lg/Md/Sm):** Regular (400). High-readability for court descriptions and rules.
*   **Label (Md/Sm):** Medium (500), All-Caps. Used for metadata (e.g., "MATCH TYPE," "AVAILABILITY").

---

## 4. Elevation & Depth: The Layering Principle
Forget traditional drop shadows. Hierarchy is achieved through **Tonal Layering** and physical stacking.

*   **The Stack:** 
    *   **Level 0 (Base):** `surface` (#0e0e0e).
    *   **Level 1 (Sections):** `surface-container-low`.
    *   **Level 2 (Cards):** `surface-container-highest`.
*   **Ambient Shadows:** For floating elements (Modals/Action Sheets), use an ultra-diffused shadow: `rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.4)` with a 40px blur and 0px offset. It should feel like an atmospheric glow, not a hard shadow.
*   **The Glass Fallback:** For elements passing over vibrant content (like a header over a court photo), use a glassmorphism effect: `surface-container` + `blur(12px)`.

## 5. Components

### Buttons
*   **Primary:** `rounded-full`, Background: `Primary Kinetic Gradient`, Text: `on-primary` (Deep Olive). Bold weight.
*   **Secondary:** `rounded-full`, Background: `surface-container-highest`, Text: `on-surface`. No border.
*   **Tertiary:** Text only, `primary` color, Semi-Bold, All-Caps.

### Chips (Filters & Tags)
*   **State - Default:** `surface-container-high`, `rounded-full`, white text.
*   **State - Selected:** `primary` background, `on-primary` text. Use a slight "pop" scale animation (1.05x) on selection.

### Cards (The "Editorial Tile")
*   **Rule:** Forbid divider lines.
*   **Style:** `rounded-lg` (2rem) or `rounded-xl` (3rem). Use image backgrounds with a `surface-container-lowest` gradient overlay at the bottom for text legibility.
*   **Interaction:** On hover/touch, the card should scale slightly and the `primary` color should bleed into the label.

### Input Fields
*   **Style:** `rounded-full`, Background: `surface-container-low`.
*   **Active State:** Background shifts to `surface-variant`. A subtle "Ghost Border" (outline-variant at 20% opacity) may be used only here.

### Context-Specific Components
*   **The Momentum Bar:** A custom progress indicator for match bookings using the `secondary` to `primary` gradient.
*   **Court Visualizer:** A glassmorphic overlay that sits on top of court maps/images to show availability.

---

## 6. Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
*   **Do** use asymmetrical layouts (e.g., a large headline on the left, a small label on the right).
*   **Do** use `rounded-full` for all interactive elements to maintain the "Playtomic" premium feel.
*   **Do** lean into high-contrast "Big Type" for empty states.

### Don't:
*   **Don't** use 1px lines/dividers. Separate content with space or color shifts.
*   **Don't** use standard "Material" blue or "iOS" default grays. Every gray must be a "Charcoal" (`#121212` derivatives).
*   **Don't** use sharp corners. If it's not rounded, it's not part of the system.
*   **Don't** use pure black (#000000) for backgrounds; keep it at `#0e0e0e` to allow for "lower" depth tiers.
