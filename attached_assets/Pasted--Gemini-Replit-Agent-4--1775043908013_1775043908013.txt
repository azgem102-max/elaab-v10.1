🚀 System Architect Brief: Project "El'ab" (إلعب)
Role: You are a Senior Full-stack Developer and Product Architect. Your mission is to build "El'ab", a sports community ecosystem for the Saudi market.

🌟 1. The Core Problem & Solution
The Problem: Amateur sports in Saudi Arabia (Football, Padel, Tennis) suffer from "flaky" players (no-shows), disorganized WhatsApp groups, and messy payment tracking (Gatta).

The Solution: "El'ab" is a Trust-First platform. It uses a proprietary Reliability Engine and a Manual Financial Ledger to ensure only committed players stay in the community.

⚖️ 2. The Engine (Logic Pillars)
You must design the app architecture around these two non-negotiable logic engines:

The Reliability Engine: * New Users: Any user with < 3 matches is marked as "New" (جديد) with a grey badge.

Established Users: After 3 matches, a 0-100% score is calculated based on attendance and peer ratings.

Penalty Logic: Automatic deductions for no-shows or late cancellations (< 2 hours).

The Gatta (Financial) Ledger:

The Match Organizer is the "Master of the Session."

They have manual toggle authority over: [Attendance Status] and [Payment Status].

The system must sync this state in real-time for transparency.

🎨 3. Design Language & Identity
Aesthetic: "Warm Arena" - Minimalist, high-efficiency UX.

Color Palette: Primary Background #F5F5DC (Warm Beige). Accents: Forest Green (Football), Sky Blue (Padel), Clay Orange (Tennis).

Localization: Arabic (RTL) is the primary and default layout. RTL integrity must be maintained across all navigation and components.

Mapping: Integrate Real Google Maps API for discovery and location picking. No abstract maps.

🏗️ 4. Architectural Requirements
Structural Freedom: Do not limit yourself to a specific number of screens. Build the optimal flow that covers:

Discovery: Finding matches via list and map.

Engagement: Joining matches and selecting Tactical Positions (unique layouts for each sport).

Organization: Tools for hosts to manage requests, payments, and attendance.

Identity: Athlete profiles with Skill Radars, Reliability Badges, and Match History.

Social: Post-match rating system (Artist, Rock, Bolt) to build player reputations.

Tech Stack: Use a modern full-stack approach (e.g., React/Next.js/Expo) with a relational database (Supabase is preferred for RLS and Real-time).

🏁 5. Your First Goal
Analyze the "Golden Thread" of the user journey: from a user opening the app, finding a match, committing to a tactical position, the organizer marking them as "Paid", to the final post-match rating. Ensure every state change is logically connected and persistent.
