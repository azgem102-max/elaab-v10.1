# Design System Strategy: Kinetic Editorial

## 1. Overview & Creative North Star
**Creative North Star: "The Kinetic Pulse"**

This design system moves beyond the utility of a standard booking tool to create a premium, high-energy sports ecosystem. We are rejecting the "flat grid" in favor of **Kinetic Editorial**—a style that combines the high-contrast impact of a luxury sports magazine with the fluid, layered depth of modern digital interfaces.

To break the "template" look, we utilize:
*   **Intentional Asymmetry:** Overlapping elements and "floating" content blocks that defy rigid container boundaries.
*   **Athletic Contrast:** Pairing deep, nocturnal foundations (`surface`) with electric, neon highlights (`primary_fixed`) to mimic the feeling of a floodlit stadium at night.
*   **Tonal Depth:** Replacing structural borders with a hierarchy of nested "light-emitting" surfaces.

---

## 2. Colors & Surface Philosophy

### The Foundation
The palette is anchored by the deep navy `#00103a`. This is not just a background; it is the "canvas" from which all energy radiates.

*   **Primary Accent (`#c1f422`):** Reserved strictly for "The Win"—key conversions, CTA buttons, and high-impact status updates. 
*   **Secondary/Action (`#2C54E8`):** Used for navigation, links, and secondary interactive elements to provide a professional, athletic contrast.

### The "No-Line" Rule
**Explicit Instruction:** Designers are prohibited from using 1px solid borders for sectioning or containment. 
*   Boundaries must be defined solely through background color shifts.
*   A card sitting on the `surface` must use `surface_container_low` or `surface_container_high` to define its shape.
*   This creates a "molded" look rather than a "pasted" look.

### Surface Hierarchy & Nesting
Treat the UI as a series of physical layers. 
1.  **Base:** `surface` (#00103a)
2.  **Sectioning:** `surface_container_low` (#051845)
3.  **Interactive Cards:** `surface_container_high` (#172754)
4.  **Floating Elements:** `surface_bright` (#273764)

### The "Glass & Gradient" Rule
To elevate the premium feel, use **Glassmorphism** for navigation bars and floating action menus. 
*   **Effect:** Apply `surface_variant` at 60% opacity with a `20px` backdrop-blur. 
*   **Signature Textures:** For main CTAs, use a subtle linear gradient from `primary_fixed` (#c1f422) to `primary_fixed_dim` (#a7d700) at a 135-degree angle. This adds "soul" and volume to the button, preventing it from feeling like a flat vector.

---

## 3. Typography: Bold Precision

We use **Inter** as our sole typeface, relying on extreme weight variations to create editorial rhythm.

*   **Display & Headline:** Use `display-lg` and `headline-lg` with tight tracking (-0.02em) and a heavy weight. These should feel like headlines in a premium sports periodical—authoritative and loud.
*   **Functional Text:** `body-md` and `label-md` are for utility. Ensure a minimum line height of 1.5 to maintain accessibility against the high-contrast dark background.
*   **Hierarchy Tip:** Contrast `on_primary_fixed` (dark text) against `primary_fixed` (neon background) for the most critical actions. All other text uses the `on_surface` or `on_surface_variant` tokens for a sophisticated, legible grey-scale.

---

## 4. Elevation & Depth

### The Layering Principle
Depth is achieved through **Tonal Layering**. For example, a court booking card should not have a border; it should be a `surface_container_highest` block resting on a `surface_container_low` section.

### Ambient Shadows
When an element must "float" (e.g., a modal or a floating action button):
*   **Opacity:** 4% to 8%.
*   **Blur:** Extra-diffused (30px–60px).
*   **Color:** Use a tinted shadow (`#000b2e`) rather than pure black to maintain the color integrity of the navy background.

### The "Ghost Border" Fallback
If accessibility requirements demand a border, use a **Ghost Border**: 
*   Token: `outline_variant` (#444934).
*   Opacity: 15%. 
*   This provides a hint of a boundary without "cutting" the editorial flow.

---

## 5. Components

### Buttons
*   **Primary:** Rounded `full`, background `primary_fixed`, text `on_primary_fixed`. No border. Subtle 135° gradient.
*   **Secondary:** Rounded `full`, background `secondary_container`, text `on_secondary_container`.
*   **Tertiary:** No background. Text `on_surface`. Underline on hover only.

### Input Fields
*   **Style:** `surface_container_highest` background with a `full` or `md` corner radius. 
*   **Interaction:** On focus, the background transitions to `surface_bright` and a `primary_fixed` ghost border (20% opacity) appears.

### Cards & Lists
*   **The Divider Ban:** Strictly forbid horizontal 1px lines. 
*   **Spacing:** Separate list items using `1rem` of vertical whitespace or alternating background tints between `surface_container_low` and `surface_container_high`.

### Athletic Progress Components (Custom)
*   **The Pulse Ring:** Use for availability or level indicators. A semi-transparent `secondary` ring with a solid `primary` segment.
*   **Match Chips:** Selection chips using `full` roundedness and `surface_variant` background, moving to `primary_fixed` on selection.

---

## 6. Do's and Don'ts

### Do
*   **Do** use massive "Display" type to anchor page sections.
*   **Do** overlap images over container edges to create a sense of movement.
*   **Do** use `primary_fixed` (neon) sparingly to guide the eye; if everything is neon, nothing is.
*   **Do** use `backdrop-blur` on headers to keep the content feeling integrated as the user scrolls.

### Don't
*   **Don't** use 1px solid borders (except the 15% opacity Ghost Border).
*   **Don't** use pure black (#000000) for shadows or backgrounds; stay within the navy spectrum.
*   **Don't** use standard "drop shadows" with high opacity.
*   **Don't** cram content; use the `xl` spacing scale to let the high-impact typography breathe.
