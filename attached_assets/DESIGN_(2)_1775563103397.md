# Design System: The Warm Arena / Digital Majlis

## 1. Overview & Creative North Star
The Creative North Star for this design system is **"The Digital Majlis."** 

In traditional architecture, a Majlis is a space of community, warmth, and hospitality. We translate this into a digital experience by moving away from the cold, rigid grids of standard athletic apps toward a "Warm Arena." We prioritize an editorial high-end aesthetic that feels like a premium sports magazine rather than a utility tool.

The system breaks the "template" look through:
*   **Intentional Asymmetry:** Hero elements and abstract silhouettes are placed off-center to create a sense of kinetic movement.
*   **Overlapping Surfaces:** Elements bleed into one another using glassmorphism, creating a sense of physical depth.
*   **Editorial Hierarchy:** Extreme contrast between massive `display-lg` headlines and tight, functional `label-sm` metadata.

---

## 2. Colors & The "No-Line" Philosophy

### The Palette
The palette is rooted in earthy tones that evoke the desert sands and clay courts, punctuated by high-chroma sport-specific accents.

*   **Primary (Football):** `#1B6D24` (Deep Turf) | Container: `#D4FFCB`
*   **Secondary (Padel):** `#00639A` (Deep Sky) | Container: `#51B2FE`
*   **Tertiary (Tennis):** `#9C4400` (Clay) | Container: `#FFDBCA`
*   **Neutral Foundation:** Base Background `#F5F5DC` (Warm Beige), Surface `#FBFBE2`.

### The "No-Line" Rule
**Prohibit 1px solid borders for sectioning.** 
Structural separation is achieved exclusively through:
1.  **Tonal Shifts:** Placing a `surface-container-low` card on a `surface` background.
2.  **Negative Space:** Using the spacing scale to create breathable gutters.
3.  **Shadow Depth:** Using soft ambient light to lift an element.

### Surface Hierarchy & Nesting
Treat the UI as a series of stacked sheets of fine paper or frosted glass.
*   **Base:** `surface` (#FBFBE2) - The floor of the Majlis.
*   **Level 1:** `surface-container-low` (#F5F5DC) - For secondary content areas.
*   **Level 2:** `surface-container-highest` (#E4E4CC) - For active, tappable card components.

### The "Glass & Gradient" Rule
To achieve "Liquid Glass 2026," use `backdrop-blur (20px-40px)` on floating headers and navigation bars. Use subtle gradients for CTAs, transitioning from `primary` to `primary_fixed_dim` to simulate a tactile, curved surface.

---

## 3. Typography
We use a bilingual pairing: **Cairo** for the rhythmic beauty of Arabic and **Inter** for the precision of English.

*   **Display (Editorial Impact):** Use `display-lg` (3.5rem) for player stats or "Join" calls to action. Maintain tight letter-spacing (-2%) for a premium feel.
*   **Headline (Sectioning):** `headline-md` (1.75rem) serves as the primary navigation anchor.
*   **Body (Readability):** `body-lg` (1rem) for descriptions. Ensure a line-height of 1.6 to maintain the "breathable" feel.
*   **Labels (The "Utility" Layer):** `label-sm` (0.6875rem) in all-caps (English) or bold (Arabic) for match times and court numbers.

---

## 4. Elevation & Depth

### The Layering Principle
Do not use shadows to define every card. Instead, "nest" surfaces. 
*   *Example:* A sport-filter chip should be a `surface-container-highest` element sitting on a `surface-container-low` header.

### Ambient Shadows
When an element must "float" (e.g., a Floating Action Button or a Modal), use the following shadow spec:
*   **Blur:** 40px to 60px.
*   **Opacity:** 4% - 8%.
*   **Color:** Tinted with `on-surface` (#1B1D0E) to avoid a "dirty" grey look.

### Glassmorphism & Depth
For the "Warm Arena" feel, use semi-transparent fills on containers:
*   **Fill:** `rgba(251, 251, 226, 0.6)`
*   **Effect:** Backdrop Blur 30px.
*   **Ghost Border:** If contrast is low, use a 1px "Ghost Border" using `outline-variant` at 15% opacity.

---

## 5. Components

### Tactile Gradient Buttons
Buttons are not flat boxes; they are physical triggers.
*   **Primary:** Gradient from `primary` to `primary_fixed_dim`. Radius: `xl` (3rem).
*   **States:** On press, the gradient reverses or darkens by 10%.
*   **Typography:** `title-sm` centered, high-contrast `on-primary`.

### Breathable Lists
*   **Rule:** No dividers. 
*   **Separation:** Each list item is a "cell" with a `surface-container-low` background on hover/active.
*   **Spacing:** 16px vertical padding between items to ensure the "Editorial" look.

### Input Fields
*   **Style:** Minimalist. No bottom line.
*   **Container:** `surface-container-highest` with a `lg` (2rem) corner radius.
*   **Focus:** Instead of a border, the background shifts to a slightly lighter tint, and the label moves into a `label-sm` editorial position.

### Abstract Vector Silhouettes
Instead of generic stock photos, use abstract, organic vector shapes of athletes in motion. These should be rendered in `surface-variant` with 30% opacity, sitting behind text to add texture without clutter.

---

## 6. Do’s and Don’ts

### Do:
*   **Do** embrace RTL-first design. Ensure Cairo’s ligatures have room to breathe.
*   **Do** use `xl` (3rem) border-radii for main containers to mimic the "Liquid Glass" softness.
*   **Do** use sport-specific accents as "pills" of color to categorize content at a glance.

### Don’t:
*   **Don’t** use 1px solid black or grey lines. They break the "Warm Arena" immersion.
*   **Don’t** crowd the screen. If you feel like you need a divider, you actually need more whitespace.
*   **Don’t** use standard "drop shadows." They feel dated. Use ambient, wide-spread glows.
